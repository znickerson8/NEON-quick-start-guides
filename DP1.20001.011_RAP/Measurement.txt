"The Rapid Habitat Survey is conducted annually at NEON wadable streams. Stream habitats are delineated and measured, and dominant substrata, riparian cover percentage, and large woody debris prescence are recorded to characterie the physical attributes of the stream. Pebble count surveys are also conducted where substrate particle size is measured along five cross-sections to characterize particle size distribution. Habitat locations are recorded with a GPS receiver and data are entered using  mobile applications. "

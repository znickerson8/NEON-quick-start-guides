"Field Quality assurance (QA) is performed before field crews leave a site to catch invalid values (e.g., collection dates, location IDs) that cannot be corrected later. Office QA, conducted afterward, reviews Habitat Survey and Pebble Count mobile application records for transcription errors (e.g., a length of 1.5 m entered instead of 15.0 m), confirms that a GPS waypoint was collected at the downstream boundary of the monitoring reach, at each habitat unit tail, and at the upstream boundary of the monitoring, and confirms that each of the five designated cross-sections has exactly 40 pebble count measurements. The Pebble Count record must be linked to its corresponding Rapid Habitat Assessment record, with the eventID and surveyEndDate reconciled to the later of the two survey completion dates."

"Surveys are conducted using a robotic total station (Hilti POS-180) with a POC-100/200 hand-held datalogger. Permanent benchmark are used to establish the initial total station location.  Staff then traverse the site using foresight/backsight shots to map sampling locations finizale the surevy by mappying loop closures on permanent benchmarks.  During the survey multiple points are mapped along the cross-section of the channel where discharge is measured. The staff gauge is also mapped.  Data are entered in the field using a mobile application, with paper datasheets available as a backup; mobile devices are synced at the end of each field day where possible."

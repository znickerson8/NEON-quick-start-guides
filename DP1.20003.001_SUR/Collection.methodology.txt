"Surveys are conducted using a robotic total station (Hilti POS-180) with a POC-100/200 hand-held datalogger. Surveys begin by setting up the total station and referencing it to fixed benchmarks that are typically installed near the staff gauge and discharge cross-section. The total station is then used to map multiple points along the discharge cross-section (capturing as much of the right and left bank floodplain, the right and left banks, and the stream bed as possible) and a single point on the staff gauge. Data are entered in the field using a mobile application, with paper datasheets available as a backup; mobile devices are synced at the end of each field day where possible."

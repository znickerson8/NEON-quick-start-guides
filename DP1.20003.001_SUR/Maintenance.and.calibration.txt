"A field calibration of the total station is required prior to the beginning of each AIS site survey. Once an initial field calibration has been performed for a given deployment, it does not need to be repeated for the remainder of that survey unless the instrument has been dropped, potentially damaged, or is exhibiting signs of malfunction. Factory calibration of the total station occurs on a less frequent basis than field calibration: it is required within 1 year leading up to a stream morphology survey, and at minimum every 5 years regardless of field calibration outcomes. Factory calibration is also required any time the instrument fails the field calibration check, or before an initial 2-year warranty expires."

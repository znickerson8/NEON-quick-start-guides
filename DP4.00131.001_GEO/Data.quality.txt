"Field Quality assurance (QA) is performed during the survey to catch invalid values that cannot be corrected later. Office QA, conducted afterward, reviews mobile application records for transcription errors (e.g., a length of 1.5 m entered instead of 15.0 m), and confirms that each of the five designated cross-sections has exactly 40 pebble count measurements. Data entered in the mobile application are carefully reviewed upon survey completion, including the ""mapped points that contain errors"" table, which documents points needing edits or deletion during post-processing.
